The rule is scoped to the nondegenerate isolated q2 band-3 eigenvalue, identical rigid motifs, primitive translation symmetry at A=0, and differentiable shape tangent. It does not cover degenerate subspaces, motif/material deformation, local or aperiodic deformation, Berry/transport quantities, or R12. Global gap maxima are diagnostics only; closure uses the band-3-specific budget.
