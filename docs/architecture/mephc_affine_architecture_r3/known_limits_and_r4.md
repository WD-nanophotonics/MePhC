# Known Limits and R4 Candidates

- Point-group symmetry is not inferred automatically.
- The deformation is global and periodic; local motif deformation is not part
  of this contract.
- Berry curvature remains Abelian single-band curvature; no non-Abelian
  degenerate-subspace workflow is introduced.
- Current-BZ generic paths expose honest landmarks but do not claim K/M
  semantics for a deformed lattice.
- SqrLatt is a downstream hold point and was not modified in R3.
